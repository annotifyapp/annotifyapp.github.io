# Personal Knowledge System

**Notes, tools, and disciplines for continuous improvement — of systems, of work, and of self. Written in the open, dated, and free.**

I maintain this site because insight that stays private is wasted, and because writing something down is the only reliable way I know to find out whether I actually believe it.

---

## What I Hold

**Gratitude first.** I did not earn the good in my life. Gratitude is the correction that keeps judgment honest.

**Originals, not copies.** We are created to be unique, not to follow a crowd. I borrow techniques freely and imitate no one's calling.

**Subtraction over accumulation.** Possessions, opinions, and commitments all bill me in attention. Emptiness shows me what I actually need.

**Discipline earns freedom.** Chosen constraints — early hours, hard training, simple food, short sentences — buy autonomy that comfort never will.

**Reality signs off.** An idea earns its place by surviving contact with a real system, a real measurement, or a real deadline. Everything else is opinion.

**Faith orders the work.** Love of God above all, and neighbor as self, is the standard against which the rest of this site should be judged — including its author.

---

## What I Am Working On

Agentic operations that are reliable enough to be boring. Industrial and robotics systems that hold up under hostile conditions. Faith-aligned technology for habit, attention, and character. Agentic AI is a tool in all three, never the point: it compresses the work so that time returns to worship, craft, and the people in front of me.

The binding constraint is rarely access to tools. It is the rate at which practice absorbs new capability and retires habits that no longer pay. That gap is the problem I work on.

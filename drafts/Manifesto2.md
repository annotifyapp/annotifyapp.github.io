# Personal Knowledge System

**Open-source notes, tools, and disciplines for continuous improvement — of systems, of work, and of self.**

This site is a personal knowledge system (PARA + BASB) maintained in the open. It exists to answer one recurring question well: *what is the most significant problem the people doing this work are actually facing — and what have I learned that is useful to them?*

The same question works at a factory floor, in a robotics lab, and in a life. The structure below is how I keep the answer honest.

---

## The Operating Method

Four steps, applied to everything:

1. **Capture** — write it down the day it occurs, in the journal, before it is polished into something forgettable.
2. **Compress** — reduce it to a claim, a constraint, and a test. If it cannot be stated in three sentences, it is not yet understood.
3. **Validate** — dogfood it. Run it on my own workflow, my own training, my own money. Reality is the only reviewer that counts.
4. **Refactor or retire** — keep what survived contact, delete what did not. Old habits are liabilities with good reputations.

The binding constraint is almost never access to tools. It is the rate at which practice can absorb new capability and retire habits that no longer pay for themselves. Closing that gap is the work.

## Three Working Domains

| Domain | The problem I am working on | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Agentic operations** | Translating multi-agent and harness engineering into realized operational gains — reliably, repeatably, and cheaply enough to be boring. | `docs/`, `packages/`, UNPLUGistan notes |
| **Industrial & robotics systems** | Systems that hold up under heterogeneous, autonomous, remote, and hostile conditions — with causal inference and exhaustive root-cause investigation as the method rather than intuition. | HROS.dev roadmap, Ishikawa/FMEA notes |
| **Discipleship technology** | Faith-aligned tools for habit, attention, and character — measurable rather than merely admired, treating sin as a productivity defect with a root cause. | 200-module citizenship curriculum |

Agentic AI is a tool in all three. It compresses the work so that time returns to worship, craft, and the people in front of me. It is never the point.

## Five Commitments

- **Gratitude first.** I did not earn the good in my life. Gratitude is the correction that keeps judgment honest.
- **Originals, not copies.** We are created to be unique, not to follow a crowd. I borrow techniques freely and imitate no one's calling.
- **Subtraction over accumulation.** Possessions, opinions, and commitments all bill me in attention. Emptiness is how I find out what I actually need.
- **Discipline earns freedom.** Chosen constraints — early hours, hard training, simple food, short sentences — buy autonomy that comfort cannot.
- **Reality signs off.** An idea earns its place by surviving contact with a real system, a real measurement, or a real deadline. Everything else is opinion.

I invest in people who carry their own weight and build while they talk. I do not spend time on complaint that arrives without a proposed test.
